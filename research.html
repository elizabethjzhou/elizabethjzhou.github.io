PIAAs


A PIAA is a phase induced amplitude apodizer, which is an in theory, lossless wavefront apodizer that utilizes two optics (mirrors or lenses) to first reshape and then flatten the phase of any initial wavefront into a desired final wavefront. I have been working on designing such an optic for the purposes of reshaping a non-symmetrical initial wavefront to a symmetrical gaussian final wavefront. This will improve the coupling of incoming wavefront into single mode fibers and allow for better data acquisition.

DAFNs


A DAFN is a duel aperature fiber nuller, which is a high dispersion coronagraph for exoplanet detection. As exoplanets are billions of times dimmer than their host stars, instrumentation that nulls the star's light while amplifying planet light are vital. The DAFN uses the destructive interference pattern created by applying a half wavelength delay to one of two aperatures to null the star light while amplifying the planet light in the sidelobes. I have been working confirming the effectivness of this concept on a test bed for on telescope application.
